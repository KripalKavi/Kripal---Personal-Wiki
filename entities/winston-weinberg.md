# Winston Weinberg

## Role / Background

CEO and co-founder of Harvey, an AI platform for legal and professional-services work. Weinberg trained as a lawyer and co-founded Harvey after testing GPT-3 on real legal questions.

## Key Ideas

- Re-rank priorities continuously against the current company P0.
- Treat almost every decision as a reversible two-way door.
- Founders should stay close to product after distribution exists.
- AI will reshape professional workflows and institutions, not just automate isolated tasks.
- Resilience is a learnable operating capability; repeated small failures build tolerance for larger bets.

Weinberg offers a practical founder-operator view of decision velocity, executive stress propagation, and vertical AI as profession redesign.

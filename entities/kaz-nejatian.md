# Kaz Nejatian

## Role/Background

CEO of Opendoor. Previously a senior Shopify leader, Nejatian brings a founder-mode, systems-oriented approach to company turnarounds, culture, and AI-enabled operating design.

## Key Ideas

- Companies drift through comfortable lies, process accumulation, and local incentives; turnarounds must confront the defaults directly.
- Truth is more valuable than emotional comfort when the organization is losing contact with reality.
- Small teams, direct disagreement, clear ownership, and explicit mission constraints beat broad alignment rituals.
- AI can flatten management by giving each worker more context and decision capability.
- Leaders need direct exposure to customers, raw data, and the product because dashboards are only maps of the terrain.

## Relevance

Nejatian is a valuable case study in operating a company under existential pressure. His ideas are particularly relevant to large organizations trying to remove coordination layers, preserve truth in big rooms, and use AI to increase ownership rather than create another layer of management.

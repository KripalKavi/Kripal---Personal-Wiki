# Snap

## What they do

Snap is the company behind Snapchat, a camera-first messaging and consumer social product built around fast visual sharing and communication.

## Growth story

Sellis describes Snapchat as an unusually strong consumer product portfolio: camera, messaging, maps, astrology, and content products with deep penetration among younger users. Its 2018 redesign damaged usage; the recovery centered on performance, especially Android performance, and produced a multi-year growth renaissance.

## Strategic insights

- A creation-oriented camera flow and a messaging utility have different advertising physics from a feed-based consumption product.
- The product can be excellent while the monetization model remains structurally mismatched.
- A narrow core value—being the fastest way to share a moment with people you care about—can guide both roadmap choices and retention metrics.
- Snap's early PM-light operating model is a useful test for whether product management adds leverage or ceremony.

## Role / Background

Founder of the **Skip Community** (125 curated heads of product and CPOs; monthly SF gatherings) and **Skip Coach** (broader tech professional community). Creator of the **Skip Show** podcast (~50 episodes, in partnership with Lenny Rachitsky). Former CPO at Credit Karma. Longtime exec at Meta and Google. Four-time founder.

Singhal is a real-time observatory into what's actually changing at leading product organizations — his communities give him direct visibility into how CPOs across startups and late-stage companies are restructuring, hiring, and rebuilding their operating models.

## Key Ideas

- **Builder vs. Information Mover**: The definitive PM archetype split for the AI era. ~50% of PMs are information movers whose core superpower is being automated; ~50% are builders who will thrive. See [concept page](../concepts/builder-vs-information-mover.md).
- **Shadow Superpower**: The PMs who mastered the old game are the hardest to change — they have no incentive, maximum institutional validation, and mastery that creates a blind spot to the new model. See [concept page](../concepts/shadow-superpower.md).
- **Joy as Activation Energy**: The psychological threshold from fear to engagement is crossed at the first personal joy moment with new tools. Engineering that moment is the key leadership intervention for change management.
- **Skip Job Thinking**: Always optimize for the move after the next move. During transitions, prioritize trajectory over title or level.
- **Obsolescence as Engineering Ethic**: The definition of a great engineer — and now a great PM — is someone who systematically obsoletes themselves from everything they do.
- **Adults Still Valued**: Wisdom + hands-on credibility + ability to speak founders' language remains a durable profile. But only when combined with current skills, not as a substitute for them.

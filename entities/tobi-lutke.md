# Tobi Lütke

## Role/Background

Founder and CEO of Shopify. Lütke is an engineer-founder whose operating philosophy connects product quality, technical systems, organizational design, and personal learning.

## Key Ideas

- AI should improve the environment for human judgment, not obscure who is responsible for a decision.
- Strategy is hardest when several options are good and the strongest long-term path lacks immediate feedback.
- Companies, products, and people should be treated as malleable, unfinished systems that periodically need pruning and rebuilding.
- Taste is built through deep study and repeated practice; it becomes more valuable as AI makes generation abundant.
- Metrics are proxies. When a proxy becomes the objective, teams overfit and the metric stops representing the underlying goal.
- He describes failure as useful when it is non-catastrophic: it can release scarce people and attention for a better bet. His personal behavior-change practice is handwritten affirmations, including adding “yet” to fixed-sounding statements.

## Relevance

Lütke is a useful source for studying how an incumbent technology company can combine founder-level product instinct with AI-native operating practices. His distinction between information, judgment, and responsibility is especially relevant to enterprise AI governance and product leadership.

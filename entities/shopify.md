# Shopify

## What they do

Shopify provides commerce software and infrastructure for entrepreneurs and merchants. In this episode, it is also a case study in adapting an established product organization to agentic software.

## Strategic insights

- **River:** Shopify’s internal AI is presented as an AI colleague with memory, personality, and permission to challenge the CEO—a concrete example of a company-wide agent rather than a narrow feature copilot.
- **Human responsibility:** Agents can expand research and execution, but people retain the decision and accountability layer.
- **Pruning and rebuilding:** Long-lived systems must remove accumulated complexity and periodically reconstruct their core rather than only add features.
- **Malleable culture:** Shopify’s cultural values are framed as positions that keep people, products, and the organization capable of learning and changing.
- **Say the thing:** Nejatian says the disagreement norm he carried into Opendoor became part of Shopify’s lore: name the problem early, criticize the thing rather than the person, and persist until the concern is heard.

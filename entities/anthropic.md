# Anthropic

**Description**: AI safety company, maker of Claude
**Founded**: 2021 (by former OpenAI leadership including Dario and Daniela Amodei)
**Notable metric**: $1B → $19B ARR in 14 months (as of early 2026)
**Competitive set**: OpenAI (GPT/ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), Meta (Llama)

---

## What They Do

Anthropic builds large language models (LLMs) with a focus on AI safety research. Their primary product is Claude — a conversational AI assistant available via API (for developers and enterprises) and consumer app. They compete directly with OpenAI, Google, and Meta across model capability, safety, and developer ecosystem.

---

## Growth Story

Scaled from $1B to $19B ARR in approximately 14 months — one of the fastest revenue growth trajectories in enterprise software history. Key growth drivers:
- **AI coding use case**: Became a core tool for software developers, creating a research-product flywheel (model improvements → better coding assistant → more usage → richer training signal)
- **Activation-focused strategy**: Invested heavily in getting users to first real value moment rather than optimizing acquisition
- **70/30 big-bets philosophy**: Allocated disproportionately to transformational bets over incremental optimization
- **CASH**: Internal AI-powered experimentation system that automates growth experiment design and execution

---

## Strategic Insights

**Research-product flywheel**: Building a product used in the same domain as their research (AI coding) creates a self-reinforcing feedback loop that accelerates both model capability and product utility.

**Intentional friction**: Deliberately incorporates onboarding friction to filter for committed users — a counterintuitive divergence from standard PLG playbooks.

**Self-improving system**: "Claude is growing itself at this point" — the model's capabilities are deployed to improve its own adoption and growth metrics.

**Organizational bets**: Predicts PM:engineer ratios will invert industry-wide as AI multiplies per-engineer productivity.

---

## Microsoft Relevance

Anthropic is a direct competitor to Microsoft's AI portfolio:
- **vs. Azure OpenAI**: Competes for enterprise API customers
- **vs. Microsoft Copilot**: Competes for consumer and prosumer AI assistant usage
- **vs. GitHub Copilot**: Claude's AI coding focus makes it a direct competitor

Their growth strategy and product philosophy are high-signal inputs for Microsoft AI positioning. Specifically: the activation problem they've identified is the same problem Microsoft faces with Copilot adoption across M365.

# Rachel Lockett

## Role / Background

Executive coach and former HR leader at Stripe and Pinterest. Runs Lockett Coaching. Has worked at the intersection of high-growth tech culture and people development — Stripe and Pinterest gave her direct exposure to how top-tier tech organizations build and manage talent. Writes and teaches on leadership, difficult conversations, co-founder dynamics, and team design. Uses AI (Granola + per-client context folders) to extend coaching capacity between sessions.

## Key Ideas

- **Three levels of listening**: Level 1 (internal/preparing your response), Level 2 (focused on words), Level 3 (global — beneath words: tone, body language, emotional subtext). Real coaching only happens at Level 3.
- **Coaching vs. directing**: The fundamental management mode distinction. Most managers default to directing; the hidden cost is a team that never develops judgment. The right question: can this person get there with guidance?
- **GROW framework** (Goal, Reality, Options, Way Forward): Structured coaching methodology; demonstrated live with Lenny's newsletter overwhelm ("Indiana Jones boulder"). See [GROW Coaching Framework](../concepts/grow-coaching-framework.md).
- **Nonviolent Communication (NVC)**: Marshall Rosenberg's four-step model — Observations → Feelings → Needs → Request. Goal of conflict is mutual understanding, not convincing. "Stay on your side of the net" (Carol Robbins, Stanford Touchy Feely). See [Nonviolent Communication](../concepts/nonviolent-communication.md).
- **"Would I enthusiastically rehire this person?"**: Stripe's talent clarity question. Binary — full-body yes or it's a no. Cuts through sunk-cost and likeability bias.
- **Living in your gifts**: Aim for 80% of time in activities that use natural strengths. Below 60%, burnout is coming. Two-week evening energy audit (five energizing + five draining things each night) surfaces patterns. Ask 5-10 people: "When I walk in the room, what shows up?"
- **"It's no one else's job to help you live in your gifts"**: Manager's job is performance in the hired role — career navigation belongs to the individual.
- **Co-founder dynamics**: 65% of startups fail from co-founder conflict. Structural CTO/CEO tension (skeptic/depth vs. visionary/optimist). Enneagram for self-awareness. "Co-founder vows" — explicit agreements before you need them. "End it or mend it" inflection point.
- **One-Page Plan**: Alpine Investors' People First Operating Rhythm — Vision/Values | Strategic Intentions/KPIs | Annual Goals | Quarterly Goals. Linked to higher portfolio returns. If it doesn't fit on one page, the strategy isn't clear enough.
- **Conscious Leadership**: 100% responsibility vs. victim/villain/hero triad. Jerry Colonna: "How are you complicit in creating the conditions you claim you don't want?"
- **AI in coaching**: Granola for session notes + per-client folder enables pattern analysis across sessions. Builds AI-assisted support between sessions using session notes + development plan + frameworks.

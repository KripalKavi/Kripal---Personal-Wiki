# Google

## What They Do

Global technology company; core products include Search (dominant market position), Workspace (productivity suite competing with Microsoft 365), YouTube, Android, Chrome, and Google Cloud (competing with Azure). Parent company: Alphabet. AI products include Gemini, AI Overviews, AI Mode in Search, and NotebookLM.

---

## Growth Story / AI Trajectory

- Perceived as slow to respond to ChatGPT's launch (November 2022); internal "code red" declared
- AI Overviews (formerly Search Generative Experience) launched 2024 — AI-generated summaries in search results
- AI Mode shipped in under one year (per Robby Stein, VP of Product) — one of the fastest major product launches in Search history
- Finding: AI features expanded search usage rather than cannibalizing it

---

## Strategic Insights

- **Speed at scale is possible**: Google shipped a major AI product on its most sacred, highest-traffic surface in under 12 months — demonstrating that large-company AI velocity is achievable
- **AI expands rather than cannibalizes**: Google's data shows AI integration increased search utility; the fear of AI destroying existing products appears empirically wrong at Google's scale
- **Relentless improvement over launch perfection**: Google ships AI features in an imperfect state and iterates — a departure from their historically cautious approach to Search changes
- **Workspace vs. Microsoft 365**: The core competitive battle for enterprise productivity is between Google Workspace + Gemini and Microsoft 365 + Copilot

# Claire Vo

## Role / Background

Founder of ChatPRD (an AI-assisted product requirements tool, part of Lenny's Newsletter Product Pass bundle). Product leader and early AI practitioner. Writes about building AI into personal and professional workflows. By March 2026, was running a 9-agent personal AI infrastructure system via OpenClaw on a local Mac Mini.

---

## Key Ideas

- **Personal AI agent infrastructure**: Built 9 specialized agents for work and personal life using OpenClaw; interfaces via Telegram. Signal: sophisticated product thinkers are self-assembling personal AI operating systems when off-the-shelf products don't satisfy them.
- **Agent configuration via plain text**: Uses AGENTS.md, SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, TOOLS.md, USER.md files to define agent behavior, personality, capabilities, and personal context. This is the new "programming your assistant."
- **Ambient + scheduled agents**: Her workflows are mostly proactive/scheduled (pre-meeting briefings, trend monitoring, logistics) — not reactive chat. Fundamentally different usage pattern from current AI assistants.

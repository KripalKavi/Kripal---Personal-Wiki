# Harvey

## What they do

An AI platform for legal and professional-services work, applying frontier models to domain-specific workflows and expert review.

## Growth story

Harvey began with a focused capability test: real legal questions answered by GPT-3 were evaluated by practicing lawyers. The early result—three lawyers accepted 86 of 100 answers without edits—provided a concrete wedge into a highly regulated profession.

## Strategic insights

- The product opportunity is workflow and institutional redesign, not isolated task automation.
- Domain expertise and expert judgment remain part of the product's quality loop.
- In-house legal teams and law firms may need a shared AI-enabled operating model.
- AI changes the speed and quality bar for professional services, even when headcount does not immediately fall.

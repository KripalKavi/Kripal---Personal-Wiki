# Shane Parrish

## Role / Background

Founder of Farnam Street and host of The Knowledge Project, a podcast focused on decision-making, mental models, leadership, and high-performance operators.

## Key Ideas

Parrish's interviews pressure-test how experienced operators make decisions, allocate attention, handle failure, and translate mental models into repeatable systems.

## Appearances

- Host of [Winston Weinberg: Speed, Stress, and Better Decisions](../podcasts/knowledge-project-winston-weinberg-speed-stress-decisions.md)

## Relevance

The Knowledge Project is a recurring source for practical operating principles and decision frameworks relevant to product leadership.

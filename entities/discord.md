# Discord

## What they do

Discord is a communication platform centered on communities, voice, and real-time conversation, with especially deep roots in multiplayer gaming.

## Growth story

Sellis describes Discord's strongest growth move as a return to its core: becoming the best place for friends to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing multiplayer games. The team improved performance and the surrounding experience even though many gamers already technically had Discord, because penetration did not mean the product had removed every reason to stay on the edge.

## Strategic insights

- “Already has the user” is not the same as “has captured the user's full use case.”
- Core-user focus can unlock growth even in an apparently saturated segment.
- Community and utility products often monetize on a longer time horizon because subscription value compounds after habitual use.

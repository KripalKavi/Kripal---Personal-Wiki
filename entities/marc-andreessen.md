# Marc Andreessen

## Role / Background

Co-founder of Netscape (1994) and Andreessen Horowitz / a16z (2009), one of the most prominent venture capital firms in technology. Also coined "software is eating the world" (2011). Contrarian commentator on technology, AI, and society. Known for strong, often provocative takes. Information diet: X and historical books only — avoids contemporary media.

---

## Key Ideas

- **"AI is the philosopher stone"**: sand → silicon → AI → thought. The most common thing transmuted into the rarest.
- **"Don't be fungible"** (from Larry Summers): develop rare skill *combinations*. "The additive effect of being good at two things is more than double."
- **E/F-shaped career**: depth in one domain + genuine capability in 2–3 adjacent ones; use AI to teach you the adjacent skills
- **Task loss, not job loss**: jobs persist longer than tasks. Ask which tasks are changing, not whether the job survives.
- **Mexican standoff**: PM, engineer, designer each believe they can do the others' jobs with AI — they're all kind of correct. Resolution: lateral expansion.
- **Macro timing**: productivity growth at half its historical rate for 50 years + demographic collapse = AI arrives precisely when it's needed to prevent economic stagnation, not to disrupt a thriving economy
- **Bloom's 2 Sigma**: one-on-one tutoring raises outcomes by 2 standard deviations; AI makes this universally accessible
- **AGI understates it**: Human IQ caps at 160 due to biology; AI has no such cap. AI of 200, 250, 300 IQ is coming and is a good thing.
- **Use AI as teacher**: spend time asking AI to train you, not just to do things for you

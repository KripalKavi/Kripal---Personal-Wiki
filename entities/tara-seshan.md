# Tara Seshan

## Role / Background

Head of product for Codex and ChatGPT Work at OpenAI. Before OpenAI, she spent more than six years at Stripe, where she joined as one of the first five product managers, then led product at Watershed. She is also a founder and Thiel Fellow.

## Key Ideas

- **Persistent AI coworkers**: The product opportunity is not a smarter chatbot, but a durable coworker that carries context across sessions and work surfaces.
- **Steering over rowing**: Human value shifts upward as models improve. The job becomes direction-setting, calibration, and intervention at the right moments.
- **Near-term horizon planning**: Build for where the models will be in the next two to three months, not for an imagined end state.
- **Empirical PMing**: The right response to fast-moving model capability is smaller hypotheses, faster tests, and less theory theater.
- **Ambition amplification**: Better model capability increases the importance of leadership ambition and taste.
- **Multiplayer agent workflows**: Tara's view is not just "me plus my agent"; it is humans and agents collaborating together across workstreams.

# Opendoor

## What they do

Opendoor is a technology-enabled residential real-estate marketplace and market maker. Its operational challenge is coordinating a physically complex asset with fast, data-informed decisions.

## Growth story

Kaz Nejatian describes taking over when Opendoor was months from bankruptcy. The turnaround centered on eliminating bloated overhead and process, returning ownership to individual contributors, changing the company’s work defaults, and using AI to make the operating model substantially more efficient.

## Strategic insights

- **Truth as an operating constraint:** The company moved from blaming macro conditions and customers to confronting the defaults and decisions within its control.
- **AI as organizational leverage:** An AI exoskeleton around each worker reduced information and management friction, allowing more output with lower operating complexity.
- **Market-maker economics:** Opendoor’s role is to stand between parties who want to transact and reduce the friction that prevents the transaction.
- **Reality contact:** Customer conversations, site visits, raw data, and frontline observation are necessary counterweights to dashboard-driven drift.

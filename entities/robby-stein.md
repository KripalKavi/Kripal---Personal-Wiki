# Robby Stein

## Role / Background

VP of Product, Google Search. Led the development and launch of AI Mode and AI Overviews — the most significant changes to Google Search in over a decade. Previously built Instagram Stories at Instagram, the product that reversed Instagram's competitive position against Snapchat. Track record of shipping high-stakes, platform-scale products under competitive pressure and tight timelines.

---

## Key Ideas

- AI expands what users can do with a product — it doesn't cannibalize existing usage
- "Relentless improvement" as a product philosophy: transformative products improve into greatness, they rarely launch that way
- Persistence on transformative products: most teams abandon potentially game-changing work too early
- Jobs to Be Done applied at scale: the job of search is "accomplish my goal," not "find a link"
- AI Mode built in under one year through three core product principles (details paywalled)

# Peter Sellis

## Role/Background

Peter Sellis was the first product manager at Snapchat, where he spent seven years, and later served as Head of Product at Discord. His operating philosophy emphasizes taste, direct ownership, high talent density, core-product focus, and long-term compounding.

## Key Ideas

- Product teams need shared ideology and explicit decision ownership; coordination is not free.
- A PM must provide value over having no PM.
- Great organizations make room for spiky talent rather than optimizing everyone toward the median.
- Product taste is visible in what a leader refuses to ship.
- Growth usually comes from making the core product better for its existing users.
- A concise core-product-value statement should connect mission language to retention-driving metrics.
- Great PMs combine confidence with humility, process with ambiguity tolerance, and long-term thinking with urgency.

## Relevance

Sellis is a useful counterweight to process-heavy product management: his test is whether a PM sharpens judgment, speed, and product coherence enough to justify the role. His Snap and Discord examples also offer a concrete growth pattern for large platforms: recover the core habit before expanding the surface area.

# Shreya Shankar

## Role / Background

AI researcher and educator specializing in LLM evaluation, data-centric AI, and production ML systems. Co-teaches the leading AI evals course on Maven with Hamel Husain. Active on X as @sh_reya; writing at sh-reya.com. Research background in ML systems and data quality.

## Key Ideas

- LLM-as-judge evals require calibration against human judgment before they can be trusted at scale
- Theoretical saturation as a stopping criterion for error analysis — stop when new traces stop producing new failure types
- The Benevolent Dictator principle: one consistent owner per eval suite ensures reliable quality standards

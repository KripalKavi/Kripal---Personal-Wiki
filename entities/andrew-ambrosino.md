# Andrew Ambrosino

## Role / Background

Product and engineering lead for the Codex app at OpenAI. His background spans design, engineering, product management, and founding companies.

## Key Ideas

- AI makes implementation cheap; taste, curation, and judgment become more valuable.
- Product teams should prototype aggressively and revisit ideas as model capability changes.
- Product ownership should use “zone defense”: clear coverage and agency rather than exhaustive coordination.
- Roles should become more fluid without pretending that product, design, and engineering are identical.

## Relevance

Ambrosino offers a close-to-the-frontier view of how AI changes product development, team design, and the economics of prototyping.

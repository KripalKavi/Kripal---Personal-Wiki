## Role / Background

Creator of **Lenny's Newsletter** and **Lenny's Podcast** — the most widely-read/listened PM-focused newsletter and podcast in the industry (300+ episodes). Former PM and growth lead at Airbnb. Investor. Recently partnered with Nikhyl Singhal's Skip Show. Publishes periodic market reports on PM hiring, compensation, and role trends.

## Key Ideas

- Consistent advocate that PM judgment is the durable skill in the AI era — not raw building speed
- The PM role is experiencing a bifurcation: judgment/builder PMs are more in demand than ever, while information-movement PMs are at risk
- Curates and triangulates PM intelligence from top practitioners across companies; his framing and follow-up questions often surface the most actionable insights in his episodes

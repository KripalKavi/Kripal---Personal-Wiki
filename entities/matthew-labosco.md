# Matthew LaBosco

## Role / Background

Executive coach focused on nervous-system regulation. Self-describes (in the cited X thread) as having spent 20+ years coaching executives who had cycled through pills, supplements, sleep trackers, and mouth tape without resolution. His thesis: most executive sleep dysfunction is downstream of an unregulated nervous system, not a hygiene problem — and the fix is *teaching the body it is safe*, not adding another intervention.

Operates a paid workshop and content business; X-thread output is engagement-optimized and the underlying claims should be read accordingly (mixture of well-grounded physiology and coaching-register aphorisms).

---

## Key Ideas

- **Sleep is a safety problem, not a hygiene problem.** Sleeping pills sedate, mouth tape addresses symptom, supplements patch — none teach the nervous system that it can stop standing guard. The root intervention is downstream of the threat-response itself.
- **Calibration over reduction.** The fix for executive overload is not less stimulus (impossible in a tech leadership role) but calibrated response — distinguishing ant-scale inbound from break-in-scale inbound. See [Ant vs. Break-in](../concepts/ant-vs-break-in.md).
- **"Should" as the nervous-system curse word.** Resentment-laced obligation registers as ongoing threat; a day full of *shoulds* = a night full of cortisol. Maps directly onto Schoening's psychological agency thesis ([Cultivating Agency](../concepts/cultivating-agency.md)) at the physiological layer.
- **Morning cortisol *peak* enables evening cortisol *crash*.** Counterintuitive but well-grounded: the goal is a sharp morning sunlight-driven cortisol spike so the curve can hand off to melatonin at night. Flat curves don't crash on schedule.
- **3 AM wake-up = blood-sugar mechanism.** Nighttime glucose dips trigger adrenaline/cortisol to compensate, with wakefulness as side effect. Stable evening blood sugar reduces the trigger.
- **Sensitivity is data; suppression is the threat program.** Push-through culture compounds the very state it tries to override. Suppression states harden into a suppression baseline (consistent with [Davidson](richie-davidson.md)'s states-become-traits framing).

## Relevance

Worth following selectively. LaBosco's value is not original neuroscience — he's translating well-established findings (circadian cortisol, Zeigarnik effects, somatic-trauma frameworks) into prescriptions calibrated for high-performing executives. The mental models that carry over cleanly to PM leadership — calibration over reduction, "should" as physiological cost, suppression as state — are useful. The marketing rhetoric (e.g., "ages you faster than smoking or cancer") should be filtered out. For a Microsoft senior leader, his framing is most useful as the *physiological complement* to the contemplative-neuroscience track already represented in the wiki ([Davidson](richie-davidson.md)).

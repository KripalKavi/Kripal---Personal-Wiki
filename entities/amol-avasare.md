---

## Key Ideas

- **Activation as the central AI growth problem**: Getting users to first genuine value moment is more important than acquisition in AI products
- **Big bets over incremental experiments**: 70/30 allocation toward major initiatives; considers this the inverse of how most growth teams operate
- **CASH — AI-automated experimentation**: Designed an internal Claude-powered system that automates growth experiment design and execution, removing the manual overhead from the experimentation loop
- **PM:engineer ratio inversion**: Predicts the optimal ratio will flip (more PMs than engineers) as AI multiplies per-engineer productivity — product judgment becomes the scarce resource
- **Intentional onboarding friction**: Argues deliberate friction filters for committed users and improves activation quality even at the cost of sign-up volume

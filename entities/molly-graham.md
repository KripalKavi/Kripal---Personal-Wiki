# Molly Graham

## Role/Background

Leadership operator and coach with experience at Google, Facebook, Quip, and the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative. She runs Glue Club, hosts TED's *WorkLife*, and writes *Lessons*. She is known for the “Give Away Your Legos” framework for growing careers inside rapidly scaling organizations.

## Key Ideas

- Give away projects, teams, and responsibilities as the organization grows so you can create room for the next challenge.
- In the AI era, distinguish delegation to a human from delegation to a machine: AI may do the work while leaving the human with oversight and accountability.
- Normalize grief, loneliness, and burnout as real responses to major changes in the shape of work.
- Build agency by helping people design the future of their profession rather than protect the past.
- Learning velocity matters more than static expertise in a fast-changing environment.

Graham is useful for leaders translating AI adoption into humane operating models.

# Tesla

## What they do

Tesla designs and manufactures electric vehicles and energy products. In this episode, it serves as a case study in high-speed problem solving, direct customer feedback, and simplifying operations before automation.

## Strategic Insights

- Factory-floor observation can reveal a simpler constraint than weeks of abstract analysis.
- Speed is an organizational capability built from short feedback loops and fast escalation.
- Product quality can substitute for large marketing budgets when customers become the distribution channel.
- Automation is most powerful after the underlying process has been simplified and proven.

Answer a question using the personal knowledge wiki.

## Context

This wiki belongs to a Senior Director of Product Management at Microsoft. Answers should be specific, actionable, and grounded in what the wiki actually contains — not general knowledge.

## Instructions

**Step 1 — Read `index.md`**
Read the index first to understand what's in the wiki and identify which pages are most likely relevant to the question.

**Step 2 — Read relevant pages**
Based on the index, read the pages most likely to contain useful information. Prioritize:
- Concept pages (most likely to contain synthesized frameworks)
- Podcast/essay pages (most likely to contain specific examples and quotes)
- Entity pages (if the question is about a specific person or company)

Read broadly before synthesizing — a question about growth strategy might connect podcast, concept, and entity pages.

**Step 3 — Synthesize and answer**
Provide a direct, structured answer that includes:
- The actual insight, not just a pointer to where it lives
- Citations to specific wiki pages using markdown links
- Specific frameworks, quotes, and examples drawn from the pages
- Your assessment of confidence: **High** (wiki covers this well), **Medium** (partial coverage), **Low** (wiki has limited relevant content)

**Step 4 — Identify gaps**
If the question reveals a topic not well covered in the wiki, note it explicitly:
> **Gap**: [Topic] not yet in wiki. Suggested source to ingest: [recommendation if you have one].

**Step 5 — File valuable new connections**
If answering the question reveals a non-obvious connection, synthesis, or comparison between wiki pages that isn't already captured:
- Add a "Connections" or "Related Analysis" section to the most relevant existing page, OR
- Create a new concept page if the synthesis is substantial enough
- Then update `index.md` if a new page was created
- Append to `log.md`: `[YYYY-MM-DD] QUERY [Question summary]. New connections filed: [pages updated or created].`

Only log if new content was actually added to the wiki.

## Quality bar

- Answer the question directly — don't summarize what pages exist, synthesize what they say
- Be explicit about what the wiki covers well vs. poorly
- If the wiki doesn't have enough to answer well, say so and suggest what to ingest
- If the answer would be significantly improved by additional sources, name them specifically

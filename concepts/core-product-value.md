# Core Product Value

## Definition

Core Product Value is a concise statement of the essential user job that makes people return, paired with metrics that operationalize the statement and demonstrate its relationship to retention. It is both a product story and a measurement system.

## Why It Matters

A mission statement without metrics is inspirational but hard to prioritize against. Metrics without a simple story are precise but hard to align an organization around. Core Product Value joins the two: the phrase tells teams what they are protecting; the metrics tell them whether the product is actually delivering it.

## Examples

- **Snapchat**: the fastest way to share a moment with the people you care about. The phrase can decompose into speed, camera-first sharing, and relationship focus.
- **Discord**: the best way to talk and hang out with friends before, during, and after playing games. The phrase points the team toward the full gaming context, not only voice chat.

## Operating Pattern

1. Write the product's core value in plain language.
2. Break each meaningful word into observable product behaviors.
3. Identify the metrics that are causally connected to users returning.
4. Use the statement in roadmap reviews, onboarding, and trade-off decisions.
5. Revisit adjacent bets only after asking whether they strengthen the core or distract from it.

## PM Application

For Microsoft products, require each Copilot surface to name the recurring job that earns the user's return. “AI assistant” is a category description, not core value. A better statement should tell the team what to make faster, clearer, or more valuable, and which repeated behavior proves that the product is delivering.

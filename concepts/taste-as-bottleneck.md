# Taste as Bottleneck

## Definition

When AI makes implementation abundant, the limiting capability moves to taste: the judgment to choose what to build, shape it coherently, recognize quality, and know when it is ready.

## Why It Matters

The old product bottleneck was often execution capacity. AI can now produce many plausible implementations, which creates a selection and quality problem. More output without better judgment produces noise, incoherent products, and expensive rework.

## Examples

- A team prototypes ten ideas and invests in the one whose user value is obvious, rather than polishing the first feasible implementation.
- A PM evaluates a feature by its end-to-end feel and usefulness, not by whether the underlying code works.
- A general AI work surface coordinates specialized tools instead of rebuilding every tool itself.

## PM Application

For Microsoft product teams:

1. Add product-judgment exercises to hiring and promotion loops.
2. Make quality bars concrete through examples, prototypes, and user outcomes.
3. Give high-agency people ownership zones so judgment is exercised close to the work.
4. Review portfolios of prototypes, not only committed roadmap items.

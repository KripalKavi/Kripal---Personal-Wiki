# Truth Over Feelings

## Definition

**Truth over feelings** is an operating principle that gives accurate contact with reality, mission alignment, and useful disagreement priority over comfort, politeness, and avoiding offense. It is not permission to be cruel; the target is the work and the system, not a person’s dignity.

## Why It Matters

Organizations drift when people tell each other small, socially safe lies: the chart is fine, the process is necessary, the strategy is working, or the problem is caused entirely by the outside world. The larger the room, the more truth gets compressed. A turnaround therefore needs structures that make reality easier to say and harder to ignore.

## Examples

- “Say the thing”: say the problem as soon as you see it, about the thing rather than the person, and repeat it until it has been heard.
- Meetings should create disagreement and decisions, not compensate for missing context. If a meeting is only context-sharing, improve the information system.
- “The map is not the terrain”: dashboards and models are derivatives of facts. Leaders need regular customer contact, product usage, raw-data inspection, and frontline observation.

## PM Application

For Microsoft product organizations:

1. Add a truth check to reviews: what are we pretending is working, which uncomfortable fact would change the recommendation, and who has direct contact with it?
2. Separate disagreement with the product from disagreement with the person. Make dissent a required contribution when the stakes are high.
3. Limit executive distance from reality with recurring customer calls, direct product usage, and raw telemetry reviews.
4. Treat “alignment” as suspect when it arrives before real alternatives and objections have been surfaced.

# Build and Bake

## Definition

Prototype promising, model-dependent product ideas early; keep the useful artifacts alive and revisit them as model capability and user readiness improve.

## Why It Matters

In fast-moving AI markets, a failed launch may reflect capability timing rather than a bad product idea. Discarding the idea loses learning and optionality. The disciplined alternative is to prototype, identify the capability bottleneck, and return to the idea after meaningful model improvements.

## Examples

- Test a feature across model releases while keeping its intended product shape stable.
- Separate “the idea is wrong” from “the model is not ready.”
- Avoid shipping a form factor whose promise depends on capabilities users cannot yet experience reliably.

## PM Application

For Microsoft AI product teams:

1. Tag roadmap bets whose viability depends on model capability.
2. Preserve lightweight prototypes and define explicit revisit triggers.
3. Record whether a failure was caused by value, usability, reliability, or model readiness.
4. Re-test shelved ideas after major model upgrades instead of assuming the old verdict still holds.
